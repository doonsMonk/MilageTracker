MilageTracker

A free Android app for tracking the fuel mileage and running costs of your cars and motorcycles.

Download the APK (v1.4)

What it does

  • Logs fuel fill-ups and works out km/l and cost per km
  • Tracks tolls, EMI, servicing and other expenses, with the total cost of each vehicle
  • Shows a month-by-month summary and estimates the fuel and cost of a trip
  • Keeps photos of fuel and service bills with each entry
  • Prints reports or saves them as PDF, and exports to CSV
  • Supports several vehicles, and light and dark themes
  • Optionally keeps a copy of your log and bill photos in your own Google Drive

Your data

Everything you enter stays on your phone. If you choose to connect Google Drive, a copy is saved in a “MilageTracker” folder in your own Drive. The developer never receives your data. See the privacy policy for details.

Requirements

Android 10 or newer. The app is installed from an APK file, not from the Play Store.
