Privacy policy

MilageTracker · Last updated 11 November 2026

MilageTracker is a personal mileage and vehicle-cost tracker. This policy explains what the app does with the information you put into it.

Summary

Your data stays on your phone and, if you turn on Google Drive sync, in your own Google Drive. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any of it. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app stores

  • Vehicle names, types and photos you add
  • Fuel fill-ups, odometer readings, tolls, EMI and other expenses you enter
  • Photos of bills you attach
  • App settings such as the colour theme

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or using “Delete all data” removes it from the device.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off unless you tap “Connect Google Drive” and sign in with a Google account. When it is on:

  • The app asks only for the drive.file permission. This lets it create and manage the files it creates itself. It cannot see or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • It saves your log and bill photos in a “MilageTracker” folder in your own Drive, and reads them back when you restore on a phone.
  • It reads your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected. The address is kept on your device and is not sent anywhere else.
  • Data travels directly between your phone and Google. It does not pass through any server run by the developer.

You can stop syncing at any time with “Disconnect” in the app. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them in Google Drive. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app does not share your information with the developer or with any third party. Exports, backups and printed reports are created only when you ask for them and go only where you choose to save or send them.

Permissions

  • Internet – used only for Google Drive sync.
  • Camera and photos – the app opens your phone's camera or file picker when you choose to add a photo. It only receives the photo you take or pick.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/doonsMonk/MilageTracker/issues.
